Food Preference Survey

Which would you rather eat?

You will see two foods at a time. Tap the one you would rather eat. There are three food groups: rice dishes, sandwiches and wraps, and main dishes. It takes about two minutes.

  • No sign-in and no personal information is collected.
  • There are no right or wrong answers; go with your first instinct.
  • If you really cannot choose, use I can't decide to skip a pair.
